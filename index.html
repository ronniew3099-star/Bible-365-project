About IREAP

For wisdom and understanding of Scripture

Bible Study Tool

What is IREAP?

IREAP is a self-made five step reflection method that's a acrostic

The five steps

keep it simple
I
Insightful Verse(s)
What verse(s) stood out? Write them down.
R
Recap
What happened in the chapter(s)? Give a summary.
E
Examine
What does what I read teach me about God, People, or Faith?
A
Apply
How can I put what I learned into practice?
P
Pray
Respond to God in prayer based on what you read.

Example: IREAP for Ruth 1

Sample reflection
I — Insightful Verse(s)

Ruth 1:16 — Ruth's commitment to Naomi stands out: she chooses to stay with her and share her journey.

R — Recap

Naomi returns to Bethlehem after losing her husband and sons. Ruth refuses to leave her and travels with her to Bethlehem.

E — Examine

The chapter highlights loyalty, commitment, grief, and the importance of remaining faithful through difficult seasons.

A — Apply

I can practice faithful love by showing up for the people God has placed in my life, especially when circumstances are difficult.

P — Pray

God, help me show faithful love, remain present through difficult seasons, and trust You when the next step is unclear. Amen.
