Ronnie's Bible Challenge 📖

Progress, not perfection.

Ronnie's Bible Challenge is a personal Bible reading and spiritual growth application designed to help users build consistency in Scripture reading, track their progress, and deepen their relationship with God.

The app provides a structured reading schedule, daily chapter tracking, and guided reflection tools to make Bible study more intentional and sustainable.

✨ Features

Bible Reading Schedule: Follow a structured plan to read through the Bible, organized by day and assigned chapters.

Reading Progress Tracker: Mark individual chapters as completed and monitor overall progress.

Interactive Calendar: View daily reading assignments and revisit previous reading days.

Reading Carryover: Mark chapters as unable to complete and carry them forward to a future reading day.

IREAP Reflection Method: Use a guided framework to reflect on Scripture:

I – Insight: What stood out to you?

R – Recap: What happened in the chapter(s)?

E – Examine: What does what you read teach you about God, people, or faith?

A – Apply: How can you put what you learned into practice?

P – Pray: Respond to God through prayer.

Progress History: Keep track of completed readings and reflections over time.

Cloud Sync: Supabase integration supports account-based progress synchronization across devices.

Personal Growth Focus: Encourages consistency, accountability, reflection, and spiritual development.

🎯 Project Philosophy

The goal isn't simply to finish reading the Bible. It's to develop a consistent habit of engaging with Scripture, understanding God's Word, applying biblical principles, and growing spiritually.

Progress, not perfection.
